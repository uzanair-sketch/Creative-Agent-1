# Commercial Treatment: The Nitro Pulse

**Client:** Solstice Coffee Co.  
**Objective:** Launch a new nitro canned cold brew with a high-energy, premium cinematic spot.  
**Target Audience:** Urban young professionals and coffee enthusiasts aged 22-38.  
**Aesthetic Tone:** Dark, sleek, tactile, cinematic, high-contrast, moody, sophisticated.  

---

## 1. Creative Concept & Logline

> **Logline:** A sensory journey into the velvet texture of Solstice Nitro, where darkness meets the sharp, refreshing snap of nitrogen-infused coffee.

**Visual Direction:**  
High-speed phantom camera work (1000fps) to capture liquid viscosity. Lighting utilizes hard, directional rim lighting to emphasize condensation on matte black surfaces. Color grade is crushed blacks with subtle cool blue highlights and warm, rich espresso brown tones.

---

## 2. Pre-Visualization Prompts (Midjourney / Flux)

- `Macro shot of a matte black aluminum can, condensation droplets, dramatic rim lighting, dark moody studio background, 8k resolution, cinematic lighting, shot on Arri Alexa 35, 100mm macro lens --ar 16:9`
- `Extreme close up of nitro cold brew swirling in a glass, dark espresso liquid, cascading bubbles, high contrast, cinematic, sharp focus, volumetric lighting --ar 9:16`
