## 2. High-Retention Opening Hooks (0-3 Seconds)

### Hook 1: Pattern Interrupt
- **Visual Interrupt:** A hand aggressively slams a matte black Solstice can onto a solid block of clear ice, shattering it into a spray of crystal shards in 1000fps slow motion.
- **Text Overlay:** *"YOUR MORNING ROUTINE IS DEAD."*
- **Audio Hook:** Loud, hyper-crisp ice-shattering crack layered with a deep, vibrating sub-bass drop.
- **Retention Rationale:** High kinetic energy and physical destruction in the first 0.5 seconds instantly break the passive scrolling state, forcing the user to stop and look.

### Hook 2: Sensory Shock
- **Visual Interrupt:** An extreme close-up of the can tab popping open, releasing a violent, pressurized plume of nitrogen mist spraying directly at the camera lens.
- **Text Overlay:** *"STOP DRINKING FLAT COFFEE."*
- **Audio Hook:** An amplified, metallic 'SNAP' followed by an intense, lingering pneumatic hiss.
- **Retention Rationale:** The direct-to-camera spray creates a pseudo-3D depth effect that triggers a physical blink reflex, driving immediate engagement.

### Hook 3: Curiosity Gap
- **Visual Interrupt:** A reverse-motion pour where dark, velvety espresso liquid and cascading micro-bubbles defy gravity, rushing upward back into a floating matte black can.
- **Text Overlay:** *"THIS ISN'T REGULAR COLD BREW."*
- **Audio Hook:** A low-frequency, swirling vacuum sound effect that resolves into a crisp metallic click.
- **Retention Rationale:** Visual anomalies and gravity-defying physics trigger the brain's curiosity mechanism, forcing the viewer to watch longer to make sense of the image.
