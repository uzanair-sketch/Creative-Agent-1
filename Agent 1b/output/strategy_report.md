## 2. High-Retention Opening Hooks (0-3 Seconds)

### Hook 1: Pattern Interrupt
- **Visual Interrupt:** A hand slams a matte black Solstice can into a crystal ice block in 1000fps ultra slow-motion, sending sharp ice shards exploding into the frame.
- **Text Overlay:** *"STOP DRINKING WATERED-DOWN COFFEE 🚫"*
- **Audio Hook:** Ear-splitting ice shatter impact synced with a heavy sub-bass drop.
- **Retention Rationale:** Violent kinetic movement and tactile destruction instantly shock the viewer, halting feed-scrolling within 0.5 seconds.

### Hook 2: Sensory Shock
- **Visual Interrupt:** Extreme macro pull shot of a can tab popping, releasing an explosive plume of pressurized cold nitrogen mist directly into the lens.
- **Text Overlay:** *"THIS ISN'T NORMAL COFFEE ⚡"*
- **Audio Hook:** Hyper-crisp metallic snap followed by a loud, pressurized nitrogen blast.
- **Retention Rationale:** Exploits ASMR sound design and macro depth-of-field to create an visceral reaction that hooks viewers.

### Hook 3: Curiosity Gap
- **Visual Interrupt:** Reverse phantom-cam shot of velvety micro-bubbles rapidly cascading upwards, assembling into a dark coffee vortex inside a sleek glass.
- **Text Overlay:** *"WHY ARE BARISTAS ANGRY AT THIS CAN? 👀"*
- **Audio Hook:** Reversed audio vacuum swoosh snapping into instant silence.
- **Retention Rationale:** Piques curiosity through visual illusion and bold controversial hook text, driving watch time.
